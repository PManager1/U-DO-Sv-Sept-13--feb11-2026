3. Share of Orders from Subscribers in D.C.
DoorDash: Nationally, ~75% of DoorDash grocery and retail order volume comes from DashPass members. In D.C., due to dense delivery zones, that figure is estimated to be closer to 80% - 85%.

Step 1: The People Count (Statement 1)
Imagine a group of 100 people in Washington, D.C. who order groceries online:

20 people pay for DashPass.

80 people do NOT pay for DashPass (casual/occasional users).

Step 2: How Those People Behave Over a Month (Statement 2)
Now, look at how many grocery deliveries get made in a month:

The 80 non-subscribers hate paying the $5–$7 delivery fee every time. So, each of them only orders 1 time a month for an emergency.

Total orders from non-subscribers = 80 orders.

The 20 DashPass members get free delivery ($0 delivery fee). Because it's free, they use DoorDash for everything—weekly groceries, quick milk run, weekend supplies. They each order 16 times a month (4 times a week).

Total orders from DashPass members = 20 people × 16 orders = 320 orders.
