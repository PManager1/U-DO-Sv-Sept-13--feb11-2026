TaskRabbit: This is a human-to-human service marketplace. TaskRabbit does not integrate with any store's backend system. You are simply hiring a human assistant to physically walk into a building and execute manual tasks on your behalf.


== TASK RABBIT ====

Why Taskers Trust the PlatformGuaranteed Reimbursement: When you hire a Tasker, TaskRabbit pre-authorizes your credit card on file. As long as the Tasker follows the official rules (such as getting your explicit approval in the app chat and uploading a clear photo of the store receipt), TaskRabbit guarantees the payout. Even if a client's card fails later, TaskRabbit compensates the worker and deals with the collections process themselves.

Credit Card Rewards: Many experienced Taskers actually prefer using their personal cards. It allows them to rack up massive cash-back rewards or airline miles on their credit cards using other people's grocery money, which they get back in full a few days later.
